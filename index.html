Sumo Arena 3D

Party knockout game – by Ramzi Mammadli

Sumo Arena 3D is a colourful party knockout game. Push, dash and jump to stay on the arena, race through obstacle courses and survive rounds of up to 32 players – online with real players or with bots. Win the final to earn a crown and climb the weekly and monthly leaderboards.

FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. An anonymous player ID is created automatically when you first play, so your progress can be saved and backed up.

How do I play with friends?

Tap Play with friends → Create room and send the 6-character room code to your friends. They tap Join and type the code. The room owner picks the format and map; bots fill any empty places.

What happens if someone leaves or loses connection?

The match goes on for everyone else – even if the room owner leaves, another player takes the room over automatically. A player who leaves during a match is out of that match; they can come back to watch and play again from the next match.

Gem purchases

Gem packs are consumable In-App Purchases processed by Apple. Gems are added to your account as soon as the purchase completes. If a purchase does not arrive, close and reopen the game while connected to the internet – pending purchases are delivered on the next launch. If it still does not appear, contact support with the date of purchase (never send receipts or payment details).

Are items pay-to-win?

Character skins are cosmetic only. Gems can also be earned by playing (level-ups and the daily wheel).

How do I delete my data?

Contact support (link above) with your in-game name and we will delete your cloud save and leaderboard entries within 30 days. Deleting the app removes all data stored on the device.
